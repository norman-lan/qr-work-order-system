How it works
Scan & report

A resident scans the QR code on their unit, describes the issue, and sets a priority. Takes under a minute, no login required.

Route & assign

The report reaches the responsible manager immediately and gets assigned to the right vendor — a plumber, an electrician, on-site staff.

Resolve & notify

The resident gets a text and email when it's received, when work starts, and when it's done — a clear record from first report to close-out.

SMS Opt-In

About

Local Area Neighborhood's QR Work Order System is a maintenance-reporting service for residential and small commercial properties, built to replace the "call the landlord and hope it gets written down" pattern with a record that a manager, a vendor, and a tenant can all point to.

The service is currently in active development and limited testing ahead of a wider rollout. Text and email notifications are used to keep residents, managers, and vendors updated as a request moves from submitted to resolved.
