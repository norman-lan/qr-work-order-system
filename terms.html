What the service does

The service lets residents report maintenance problems at a property by scanning a QR code, and routes those reports to the responsible property manager and any assigned vendor. Residents, managers, and vendors may receive text messages and emails about the status of a request.

Text message (SMS) program

  • Program: Local Area Neighborhood maintenance request updates.
  • Consent: Residents are texted only if they enter a mobile number on the request form and check the box agreeing to receive texts. Consent is optional and is not a condition of submitting a request or of any purchase.
  • Message types: confirmation that a request was received, notice that work has started, and notice that work is completed. Managers and vendors may also receive alerts about new or assigned work orders.
  • Frequency: message frequency varies with the progress of your request, typically a few messages per request.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the texts.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out.
  • Help: reply HELP or email nlai.apartments@gmail.com.
